# Keep coaching deterministic and athlete controlled

Calculations and typed, versioned rules will produce findings and schedule-or-dose Recommendations with visible rationale, parameters, uncertainty, and provenance. Every material Recommendation requires an athlete disposition and creates a Prescription Revision only when accepted.

A future language model may explain established findings and support dialogue, but may not originate facts, bypass deterministic validation, silently change the Integrated Program, or hide insufficient evidence. Pain, illness, and suspected injury stop normal progression guidance rather than triggering diagnosis or rehabilitation advice.

This limits apparently intelligent automation, but makes guidance testable, reviewable, and reversible while preserving athlete agency and a clear safety boundary.
